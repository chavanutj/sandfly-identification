G1a | Cibarial teeth in a transverse row and pigment patch usually present (Fig. 1A). Hind end of abdominal tergites 2-6 with all or most setae recumbent, which arise from small sockets as compared with those on tergite 1 (Fig. 1B). Male style with 3 apical and 1 sub-apical long spines developed and 1 more basal setiform spine (accessory seta) (Fig. 2A). Female spermathecae smooth (Fig. 3A) | | Sergentomyia minuta | Genus Sergentomyia | The only Sergentomyia species reported in Italy.
G1b | Cibarial teeth and pigment patch usually absent (Fig. 1C). Hind end of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect setae, which arise from large sockets of the same size as those on tergite 1 (Fig. 1D). Male style with 2 long apical spines (Fig. 2B, D-H) or 3 short apical spines (Fig. 2C) and setiform spine absent. Female spermathecae with superficial striation (Fig. 3B) or annulated (Fig. 3C-H) | G2 | | Genus Phlebotomus |
