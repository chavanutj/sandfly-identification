Sergentomyia babu series: females

Female key to the four taxa with a notch at the hind end of the cibarial dental area.

SourceLewis, D.J. (1987) Phlebotomine sandflies (Diptera: Psychodidae) from the Oriental Region. Systematic Entomology 12: 163-180.
RegionOriental Region
Sexesfemale
Size3 couplets, 4 terminal taxa, 1 leads with notes (⚠)

1 Pharyngeal teeth short (scale-like and some denticulate). Cibarium with 16.7 (12-20 ±1.9) teeth and a rounded notch 15.2 (11-19 ±2.2) µm wide internally
Sergentomyia baghdadis☰
1′– Pharyngeal teeth long and pointed
→ 2
2 Cibarium with V-shaped notch and 12.6 (10-16 ±1.3) teeth
Sergentomyia shorttii☰
2′– Cibarium with rounded notch
→ 3
3 Cibarium with 25.0 (20-29 ±2.3) teeth. Wing narrow, 4.52 (4.19-5.06) times as long as wide. Notch 17.6 (11-28 ±4.1) µm wide
Sergentomyia babu babu☰
3′– Cibarium with 45.8 (42-51) teeth. Wing wide, 3.85 (3.76-3.96) times as long as wide ⚠
Sergentomyia babu insularis☰
Update from later literature
Galati et al. 2025 (p. 29) list S. babu insularis as the species S. (Parrotomyia) insularis (Theodor, 1938) (India, Sri Lanka).
