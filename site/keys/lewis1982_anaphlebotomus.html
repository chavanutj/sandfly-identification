Anaphlebotomus species (stantoni group)

Key to Anaphlebotomus. See P. shadenae (2023) for the Thai species close to P. stantoni.

SourceLewis, D.J. (1982) A taxonomic review of the genus Phlebotomus (Diptera: Psychodidae). Bulletin of the British Museum (Natural History), Entomology 45(2): 121-209.
RegionOriental, Afrotropical
Sexesboth
Size7 couplets, 4 terminal taxa, 3 leads with notes (⚠)

0 Female
→ F1
0′– Male
→ M1
F1 Spermatheca long (about eight times as long as wide) and tubular with very long duct. Afrotropical
Phlebotomus rodhaini☰
F1′– Spermatheca not long and tubular. Oriental
→ F2
F2 Spermatheca slightly carrot-shaped with small end-segment, individual duct about four (possibly more) times length of spermatheca. Sternal tubercle broad
Phlebotomus colabaensis☰
F2′– Spermatheca spindle-shaped with very narrow cylindrical apical segment, duct short but common duct very long. Ascoids long. Palp 3 with peg sensilla grouped around middle
→ F3
F3 Pharyngeal armature with antero-median numerous long pointed teeth which blend laterally with ridges. Individual ducts longer than spermathecae ⚠
Phlebotomus stantoni☰
Note: See Renaux Torres et al. 2023 on P. stantoni as a species complex
F3′– Pharyngeal armature with several antero-median rows of small short teeth, and antero-laterally a number of backward-pointing teeth. Individual ducts shorter than spermathecae ⚠
Phlebotomus hoepplii☰
Update from later literature: P. hoepplii was later treated as a synonym of P. stantoni; Renaux Torres et al. (2023) consider it valid.
M1 Paramere bilobed
Phlebotomus colabaensis☰
M1′– Paramere trilobed
→ M2
M2 Plunger of sperm pump much wider than body of barrel. Afrotropical
Phlebotomus rodhaini☰
M2′– Plunger of sperm pump narrower than body of barrel. Oriental
→ M3
M3 Spine near aedeagus not longer than it. Pharynx with a series of oblique ridges radiating from mid-line and ending in loops laterally ⚠
Phlebotomus stantoni☰
Update from later literature: P. stantoni s.l.: see P. shadenae (♂ aedeagal ducts about 655 µm versus about 304 µm in P. stantoni; Renaux Torres et al. 2023).
M3′– Spine near aedeagus much longer than it. Pharynx with a series of posterior ridges and, antero-laterally, a number of teeth projecting medio-posteriorly
