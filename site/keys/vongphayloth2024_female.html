Laos karst (Vientiane): females

Key to females of 19 species from karstic limestone caves of Vientiane Province, Laos (Vongphayloth et al. 2024, Table 4), including Se. dvoraki and Se. marolii. Couplet 9b corrected (printed goto 11); couplets 16-18 are doubtful, see the notes.

Phlebotomus breyi☰
Note: As printed; values between 120 and 130 µm are covered by neither lead, use the spermathecal neck.
Update from later literature: P. breyi (subgenus Lewisius): also f1/labrum < 1, individual spermathecal ducts > 400 µm, interocular suture incomplete (Vongphayloth et al. 2023, Table 6, p. 16). P. betisi (Malaysia; not in this key) has p4 126-141 µm and a long narrow neck, so may also reach this lead; it has ducts < 400 µm and f1/labrum > 1.

Phlebotomus sinxayarami☰
Update from later literature: P. sinxayarami (subgenus Lewisius): also interocular suture complete, f1/labrum > 1, p4 80-110 µm, individual spermathecal ducts < 400 µm (160 µm in the paratype), genital fork with short lateral apodemes (none in P. breyi) (Vongphayloth et al. 2023).
