Update from later literature
Southeast Asian males with five gonostyle spines (two terminal, upper external subapical, lower external in apical third, inner one in the middle), a simple paramere and a conical parameral sheath may belong to subgenus Lewisius (Vongphayloth et al. 2023), which is not in this key.
