Laos karst (Vientiane): males

Short key to males from karstic limestone areas of Vientiane Province, Laos (Vongphayloth et al. 2024, Table 5). Sergentomyia males are keyed to genus only.

Update from later literature
Ch. barbazani (Thailand) also has four radial veins (R2 absent; Depaquit et al. 2006). ♂ Ch. barbazani: genital filaments 177-213 µm, tip truncated, pump 125-135 µm, filaments/pump 1.36-1.6; style with four long spines in its distal half (p. 155). Outside Laos, consider Ch. barbazani.

Phlebotomus breyi☰
Update from later literature
P. breyi (subgenus Lewisius): aedeagal ducts 535.60-655.39 µm, over 4 times the sperm pump (5.03-6.25); p4 136.26-153.53 µm; interocular suture incomplete (Vongphayloth et al. 2023, Tables 5-6).
5′– Aedeagal ducts < 500 µm ⚠ Vongphayloth et al. 2024 p. 16
Phlebotomus sinxayarami☰
Update from later literature
P. sinxayarami: interocular suture complete, p4 66.40-93.46 µm, aedeagal ducts 328.91-411.75 µm (Vongphayloth et al. 2023, Table 5). P. betisi (Malaysia; not in this key) also has ducts < 500 µm (331-470) but p4 99-121 µm, an incomplete interocular suture and ascoid formula 2/f1-f6, 1/f7-f12, 0/f13-f14.
