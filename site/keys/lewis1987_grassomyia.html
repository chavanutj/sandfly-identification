Female key to all Grassomyia, showing the two Oriental taxa.

SourceLewis, D.J. (1987) Phlebotomine sandflies (Diptera: Psychodidae) from the Oriental Region. Systematic Entomology 12: 163-180.

Update from later literature
Galati et al. 2025 (pp. 27-28) treat G. dreyfussi and G. turkestanica Theodor and Mesghali, 1964 as separate species; they list G. turkestanica only from Afghanistan, Iran and Turkmenistan and put the Sri Lanka record under G. dreyfussi.
