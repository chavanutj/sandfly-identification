Genus Phlebotomus→ 2
Update from later literature
A Chinius may reach this lead: Ch. barbazani (Thailand) has no cibarial pigment patch, cibarial teeth on two lateral wings rolled backwards plus about ten punctiform central teeth, and a ♂ style with four long spines (Depaquit et al. 2006, pp. 152, 155). Genus Chinius (Depaquit et al. 2006, p. 157): in both sexes sparse setae on vertex and clypeus, eyes with few facets (< 25), complete interocular suture, short mouthparts, pharynx weakly armed posteriorly (small punctiform central teeth and a few long lateral teeth), wing rounded at tip with R2 very short (Ch. junlianensis) or absent (Ch. barbazani, Thailand); ♀ smooth spermathecae with a common duct, thick furca; ♂ trumpet glands on tergites 4 and 5, globular coxite without basal lobe or tuft of setae, shorter than the style, style with four spines, short surstyles, simple slender parameres.
