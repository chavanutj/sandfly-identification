Phlebotomine Sand Flies of the Oriental Region

Phlebotomine sand flies (Diptera: Psychodidae: Phlebotominae) transmit Leishmania, phleboviruses and Bartonella. Identification rests on microscopic characters of slide-mounted adults: the cibarium and pharynx, spermathecae and antennae of females, and the genitalia of males.

This site brings together the published dichotomous keys for the Oriental Region and updates them with later taxonomic work on Thailand and neighbouring countries.

Quick start

  1. Clear and slide-mount the specimen. Sex it (female spermathecae / male genitalia).
  2. Open Lewis 1978 and use Step through. At each couplet choose the lead that fits.
  3. Read any ⚠ note on the leads you passed: later work may have split the species.
  4. Open the species page: compare the diagnostic notes, conflicting characters and figures.
  5. Unsure of a term? See the glossary.
