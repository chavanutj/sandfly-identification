Sergentomyia siamensis Causey

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand | Records in the project regions: Laos, Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Thailand and Laos (cyt b sequences, Prasetyo et al. 2026) | Laos: karstic limestone areas of Vientiane Province (Vongphayloth et al. 2024)
ThailandPha Tong cave, Chiang Rai (Renaux Torres et al. 2023) | Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Kanchanaburi (Taklor Cave), one female separated from S. barraudi by Cytb only (Soomro et al. 2025); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesS. barraudi group; 44-54 cibarial teeth (mean 47.3) in Pha Tong material; original description 44 teeth (Renaux Torres et al. 2023). barraudi in Lewis 1978. | Galati et al. 2025 (p. 30) list it as Sergentomyia (Parrotomyia) siamensis (Causey, 1938). | Causey (1938), as summarised by Prasetyo et al. 2026: pharyngeal teeth slightly shorter than in Se. barraudi; 54 palisade-like cibarial teeth; spermathecae identical to Se. barraudi. Cambodian Se. barraudi Group 2 (more than 44 teeth) is related but differs molecularly (cyt b 0.0849). | Laos specimens: about 54 (50-54) comb-like posterior teeth, two rows of anterior teeth (about 22 and six), forked sclerotized area, f1 < 190 µm and about half the proboscis length, ovoid smooth spermathecae; Cyt b places them apart from other Se. barraudi group lineages from Thailand (Vongphayloth et al. 2024).
SourcesRenaux Torres et al. 2023, Galati et al. 2025, Prasetyo et al. 2026, Soomro et al. 2025, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2024
