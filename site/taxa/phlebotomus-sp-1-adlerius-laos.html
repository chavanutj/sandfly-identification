Phlebotomus sp. 1 (Adlerius, Laos)

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Adlerius
Statusundescribed; females only; printed as 'Ph. (Adlerius) sp.1'
DistributionLaos: Luangnamtha (2 females) - first Adlerius record from Southeast Asia outside China (Vongphayloth et al. 2026)
Diagnostic notesSpermatheca of Adlerius type (Fig 3J). Cyt-b distance 14% from Ph. chinensis from China. Adlerius females are mostly indistinguishable morphologically; males are needed before a species can be described (Vongphayloth et al. 2026). Yunnan has Ph. chinensis, Ph. fengi and Ph. sichuanensis (Leng 1997, cited there).
SourcesVongphayloth et al. 2026
