Phlebotomus mascomai Müller, Depaquit and Léger, 2007

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Euphlebotomus
Statusvalid
DistributionCaves in Thailand and Vietnam | Records in the project regions: Thailand, Vietnam (Galati et al. 2025) | Laos, first record (Vongphayloth et al. 2024) | Laos: Bokeo, Luangnamtha, Luangphabang, Vientiane province, Xiengkhouang (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, Table 2)
ThailandThai caves, incl. Pha Tong (Renaux Torres et al. 2023) | Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Type locality: cave 'Khao Tham Khun Chom', sub-district Huay Phai, district Muang, Ratchaburi Province (printed 'Ratchabun' in Muller et al. 2007; 'Ratchaburi' in Depaquit et al. 2009), 35 m a.s.l.; caught February 2005; holotype male in MNHN Paris (Muller et al. 2007). | Tham Pha Thai, Lampang; Tham Phra, Chiang Rai (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
ImportanceTrypanosoma noyesi DNA in 7 of 105 females from a Lampang cave (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024)
Diagnostic notesCavernicolous Euphlebotomus. ♀ pharyngeal armature pigmented; no accessory spine reported between parameral sheath and paramere (Ding et al. 2025). | Original description (Muller et al. 2007, Table 1, compared with P. argentipes): paramere without lateral spine, superior clasper long and narrow (P. argentipes: lateral spine, enlarged leaf-like superior clasper); ♂ ascoid formula 1/III, XIV-XV; 2/IV-XIII (P. argentipes 2/III-X; 1/XI-XV); style spines 2 distal, 2 intermediate and one between these groups (P. argentipes 2 distal and 3 intermediate); genital filaments/pump about 4 (about 2); ♀ ascoid formula 2/III-XIV; 1/XV (P. argentipes 2/III-X; 1/XI-XV); spermathecae with 20-30 rings (12-15). ♂ (n = 3): cibarium with small teeth on a regular circle, no pigment patch; pharynx 135-159 µm, poorly armed; AIII 316 µm, AIV 141 µm, AV 140 µm ('A III/A IV=1.12' as printed; the value fits AIII/(AIV+AV)); epipharynx 165-181 µm, AIII/E 1.9-1.75; coxite 189-213 µm with a cluster of 12-15 internal medio-distal setae; style 121-126 µm, coxite/style 1.70-1.93; paramere trifurcated (superior clasper 63-68 µm, middle 37 µm, inferior 8-10 µm); no spine along the aedeagus; aedeagus slender with rounded tip, 5-6 µm wide at distal third; genital filaments 480-530 µm with flared tip, pump 112-115 µm (ratio 4.3-4.6). ♀ (n = 10): pharynx 158-185 µm with typical Euphlebotomus armature; AIII 313-372 µm; epipharynx 232-287 µm, AIII/E 1.15-1.30; 2-4 mesanepisternal setae; wing 1865-2105 µm; spermatheca annulated (20-30 segments), head embedded in the thicker last segment; individual duct + spermatheca 200-280 µm, common duct 85-110 µm, total 310-365 µm. Sympatric P. barguesae differs by smooth spermathecae (♀) and a blunt, brown aedeagus (♂) (Depaquit et al. 2009). | Laos records of Ph. argentipes may be confused with Ph. mascomai (Vongphayloth et al. 2024).

Laos karst (Vientiane): females (Vongphayloth et al. 2024) – couplet 8a
  1. 1a Five radial veins on the wing
  2. 2a The longest palpal segment is the distal one (p5)
  3. 3a Abdominal tergites 2–6 with erect hairs
  4. 4b Pigmented pharyngeal armature
  5. 6b Absence of a thick and long common spermathecal duct
  6. 7b Annealed spermathecae ⚠
  7. 8a Rounded distal ring. Limit between spermathecae and ducts difficult to observe
Laos karst (Vientiane): males (Vongphayloth et al. 2024) – couplet 7a
  1. 1a Five radial veins on the wing
  2. 2a Absence of abdominal rods surrounding the aedeagal ducts
  3. 3a Five spines on the gonostyle
  4. 4b Tri-lobed paramere
  5. 6b Two ascoids on flagellomeres 2 and 3
  6. 7a Parameral sheath slender on all their length with a round top, aedeagal ducts/pump ratio > 4
