Sergentomyia sp. 2 (Soomro et al. 2025)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia
Statusundescribed
DistributionThailand (Kanchanaburi)
ThailandKanchanaburi: Phra Cave, one female (Soomro et al. 2025)
Diagnostic notesFemale only, single specimen (Soomro et al. 2025, Fig. 2): ventral plate of cibarium with two prominent cibarial (hind) teeth; pigment patch absent; spermathecae oblong, oval or pear-shaped capsules with slightly tapered, rounded apex; duct length unknown (not visible); pharynx slender with moderately wide base and a small cluster of fine spicules posteriorly. Molecular: Cytb 90.07% identity to Se. sclerosiphon, COI 88.59% to Se. perturbans. Not formally described.
SourcesSoomro et al. 2025
