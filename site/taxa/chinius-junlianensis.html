Chinius junlianensis Leng, 1987

ClassificationGenus Chinius
Statusvalid
DistributionChina: limestone caves (Leng 1987, cited in Depaquit et al. 2006; Leng's title refers to Sichuan and Guizhou Provinces, south-west China). Type species of Chinius Leng, 1987.
Diagnostic notesValues from Table I of Depaquit et al. 2006 (p. 155): ♂ antennal formula 2/III-IX 1/X-XIII (XIV); 6-9 Newstead's spines; eyes 13-15 facets; clypeus 75 µm with 5 setae; vertex with 20-25 setae; R2 short; genital filaments > 1000 µm, tip bevelled ('biseautée'); filaments/pump about 6; aedeagus with a lateral process. ♀ antennal formula 2/III-IX 1/X-XV; R2 short; spermathecae smooth, long (> 1000 µm) with a very long common duct (redescription by Depaquit et al., then in press, cited in Depaquit et al. 2006; Leng 1987 had described them erroneously). Males with trumpet glands on tergites 4 and 5. Fork of R2 (alpha) short. Compare Ch. barbazani (R2 absent, ♂ genital filaments about 200 µm with truncated tip, ♀ spermathecae about 200 µm).
SourcesDepaquit et al. 2006
