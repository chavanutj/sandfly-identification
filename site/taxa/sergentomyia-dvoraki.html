Sergentomyia dvoraki Randrianambinintsoa, Vongphayloth & Depaquit, 2024

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionLaos: Vientiane Province (type locality Pha Nok Kok cave, Feung district). Male unknown. | Laos: Bokeo, Luangnamtha, Luangphabang, Vientiane province, Xayaboury (169 females) (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, Table 2)
Diagnostic notesSe. iyengari group (Vongphayloth et al. 2024). ♀ f1 longer than f2 + f3 (f1 182-224 µm, mean 206 µm, n = 8). Cibarium with 14-16 posterior teeth (Table 2; holotype description: 18) and 2-12 anterior teeth in two central rows (holotype six bigger, six smaller); sclerotized area rounded. Spermathecae narrow and wrinkled, terminal knob deeply embedded; common duct short (29 µm), individual ducts long (255 µm) (holotype). Labrum 161-189 µm. Differs from Se. iyengari (no anterior teeth), Se. hivernus (tubular spermathecae without limit between body and duct), Se. khawi (smooth spermathecae with shorter, wider individual ducts; many anterior teeth in 2-3 rows) and Se. ashwanii (terminal knob not in a deep notch). Cyt b distance 0.12 to Se. khawi, 0.13 to Se. hivernus. Raynal's Vietnamese 'Se. iyengari' (1935) may be this species. | S. iyengari group (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, citing Vongphayloth et al. 2024).
SourcesVongphayloth et al. 2024, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
