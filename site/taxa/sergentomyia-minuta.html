Sergentomyia minuta

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionMediterranean (only Sergentomyia in Italy)

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Italy (comparison key) (Dantas-Torres et al. 2014) – couplet G1a
  1. G1a Cibarial teeth in a transverse row and pigment patch usually present (Fig. 1A). Hind end of abdominal tergites 2-6 with all or most setae recumbent, which arise from small sockets as compared with those on tergite 1 (Fig. 1B). Male style with 3 apical and 1 sub-apical long spines developed and 1 more basal setiform spine (accessory seta) (Fig. 2A). Female spermathecae smooth (Fig. 3A) ⚠
