Sergentomyia pondicherriensis Srinivasan & Jambulingam, 2010

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Sergentomyia
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: India, Sri Lanka (Galati et al. 2025)
SourcesWijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Galati et al. 2025

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Sri Lanka: females (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S12b
  1. G1b Wings narrow, lanceolate, symmetrical along the length (Fig. 1e). Cibarium with one or more rows of teeth, pigment patch usually present (Fig. 1c) ⚠
  2. S1b Spermatheca without capsule, tubular (Fig. 4d)
  3. S10a Spermatheca smooth (Fig. 4e)
  4. S11b Pharynx barrel-shaped at posterior end and lacking a deep constriction at the base (Fig. 5h, i)
  5. S12b Posterior margin of pharyngeal armature straight (Fig. 5i)
Sri Lanka: males (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S3a
  1. G1b Style with 4-5 spines, usually terminal; if not all spines terminal, 2 spines terminal and 2 sub-terminal, often in pairs (Fig. 1g) ⚠
  2. S1a Aedeagus thick, finger-shaped (Fig. 2e)
  3. S2b Style with 4 terminal spines and no subterminal spines (Fig. 2g); cibarial teeth uniform in size
  4. S3a Cibarial teeth arranged in 2 rows
