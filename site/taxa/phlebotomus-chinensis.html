Phlebotomus chinensis Newstead

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Adlerius
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: China (Galati et al. 2025) | Bhutan: first country record, at nearly all sites with Phlebotomus, 10.3% of Phlebotomus (Wangdi et al. 2024)
ImportanceCited as a proven vector of visceral leishmaniasis in China and Central Asia (Wangdi et al. 2024).
Diagnostic notesCompare Phlebotomus sp. 1 (Adlerius, Laos): cyt-b distance 14% from Chinese Ph. chinensis (Vongphayloth et al. 2026).
SourcesQuate 1962, Galati et al. 2025, Wangdi et al. 2024, Vongphayloth et al. 2026

Indo-Chinese species (Thailand to Viet Nam) (Quate 1962) – couplet 3b
  1. 1a Abdominal hairs erect, sockets on tergites 2 to 6 as large as on 1; palpal segment 4 often only 1/2 length of 3; ♂ dististyle with 2 or 3 spines in center; pharynx armed apically; cibarium at most with weak and irregularly developed teeth ⚠
  2. 2a Male dististyle with 5 spines; ♀ with antennal segment 3 ending well before tip of proboscis
  3. 3b Male paramere simple, without ventral lobes; ♀ spermatheca with distal part reduced to slender neck; ♀ cibarium with all teeth of about same size ⚠
