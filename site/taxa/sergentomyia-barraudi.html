Sergentomyia barraudi (Sinton)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Parrotomyia
Statusvalid
ThailandChiang Mai, Saraburi (Quate 1962) - as S. barraudi s.l.
ImportanceSuspected vector of Leishmania martiniquensis in Thailand (cited in Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020).
Diagnostic notesS. barraudi s.s. (India) about 40 cibarial teeth. Thai material with 44-54 teeth referred to S. siamensis; S. maiae has 57-69 teeth (Renaux Torres et al. 2023).
SourcesLewis 1978, Quate 1962, Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Renaux Torres et al. 2023
