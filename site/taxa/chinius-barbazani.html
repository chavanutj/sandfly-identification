Chinius barbazani Depaquit, Léger and Beales, 2006

ClassificationGenus Chinius
Statusvalid
DistributionThai caves only | Records in the project regions: Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Thailand: limestone caves; type locality Khao Tham Khun Chorn cave, Huay Phai sub-district, Muang district, Ratchaburi Province, altitude 35 m; also a cave at Pa Sak Gnam village, Tambon Luang Neua, Doi Saket district, Chiang Mai Province, altitude 212 m (Depaquit et al. 2006). Types (holotype ♂, two paratypes, allotype ♀) in the Muséum national d'Histoire naturelle, Paris.
ThailandPha Tong cave, Chiang Rai (Renaux Torres et al. 2023) | Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Ranong, Chumphon, Uthaithani, Lopburi, Phayao (Polseela et al. 2024); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3) | Type locality: Khao Tham Khun Chorn cave (with a Buddhist temple), Ratchaburi Province, CDC light traps 50 m inside the cave, April 2004, July 2004 and February 2005; Pa Sak Gnam cave, Doi Saket district, Chiang Mai Province, traps at the cave entrance, 19 January 2005; both sexes caught together at both sites (Depaquit et al. 2006, pp. 151-152, 155)
Diagnostic notesGenus Chinius is distinct from other Old World genera (Rispail & Léger 1998). Not in any printed key in this collection. | Ch. eunicegalatiae (Laos) also lacks r2; see that species for the differences (Vongphayloth et al. 2024). | Depaquit et al. 2006 (in French), ♂ (10 examined; pp. 152, 155): wing rounded at tip, 'quatre nervures R' (R2 absent), 1370-1500 x 405-515 µm. Antennal formula 2/III-XI 1/XII-XIV or 2/III-XII 1/XIII-XIV, sometimes a vestigial ascoid on XV; A III 170-195 µm, A IV 98-112 µm, A V 69-114 µm, A III/(A IV + A V) 0.84-0.87; epipharynx 106-117 µm, A III/E 1.6-1.75. Eyes of 18-22 facets; clypeus 98-105 µm with 6-8 setae; vertex with 35-42 setae without clear rows; interocular suture complete. Five club-shaped Newstead's spines on palp segment 3. Cibarium with lateral wings rolled backwards bearing many long thin teeth, no pigment patch; pharynx narrower than in the ♀, with five or six large teeth or lateral folds and a few posterior punctiform teeth. 40-50 trumpet glands on the antero-dorsal parts of tergites 4 and 5. Coxite globular, 150-185 x 70-80 µm, about 20 scattered ventral setae (length/width 2.34-2.52); style slender, 190-215 µm, with four long spines in its distal half (one terminal, one subterminal, two more proximal: one internal, one external); coxite/style 0.74-0.89. Paramere simple, long (165-200 µm) and slender, rounded at tip, with two or three strong distal setae; basal process barely marked. Surstyle 160-185 µm, shorter than coxite or paramere. Aedeagus ('valves péniennes') straight and thick (60-73 µm), slightly narrowed in the middle, without lateral process. Genital filaments short, 177-213 µm, tip truncated ('extrémité tronquée'); genital pump 125-135 µm; filaments/pump 1.36-1.6. ♀ (4 examined; p. 152): 1.5-1.7 mm long. Cibarium with two lateral wings rolled backwards bearing many long thin teeth reaching well into the posterior part, about ten punctiform central teeth, no pigment patch. Pharynx 150 µm long, wide, pigmented, narrowed posteriorly, with very short backward-pointing teeth in short lines and a few long lateral posterior teeth. Four or five club-shaped Newstead's spines. A III 175 µm, A IV 100 µm, A V 105 µm (A III < A IV + A V); epipharynx 150 µm, A III/E 1.16 (antennae seen on one specimen only, formula 2/III-XIII...). Eyes of about 20 facets; clypeus 97-117 µm with 7-9 setae; vertex with 35-43 setae. No setae on mesanepisternum; no spines on legs. Wing rounded, 'fusion vraisemblable des nervures R2 et R3', 1650-1850 x 580-670 µm. Spermathecae smooth, body cylindrical narrowing distally, head on a neck; individual ducts wide and short, little differentiated from the bodies, joining a common duct about 100 µm long; total length 200 µm. Spermathecae are clearly visible in Marc-André fluid but probably hard or impossible to see in other mountants: dissect the abdomen tip (pp. 152, 155-157). Differs from Ch. junlianensis (Table I, p. 155; values for Ch. junlianensis in brackets): ♂ antennal formula 2/III-XI 1/XII-XIV or 2/III-XII 1/XIII-XIV (XV) [2/III-IX 1/X-XIII (XIV)]; Newstead's spines 5 [6-9]; eyes 18-23 facets [13-15]; clypeus 100 µm with 6-8 setae [75 µm, 5 setae]; vertex 35-40 setae [20-25]; R2 absent [short]; genital filaments 200 µm, tip truncated [> 1000 µm, tip bevelled, 'biseautée']; filaments/pump about 1.5 [about 6]; aedeagus without lateral process [with]. ♀ R2 absent [short]; spermathecae short, about 200 µm [long, > 1000 µm].
SourcesRenaux Torres et al. 2023, Rispail & Léger 1998, Galati et al. 2025, Polseela et al. 2024, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2024, Depaquit et al. 2006
