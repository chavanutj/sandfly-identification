Phlebotomus seowpohi Depaquit et al., 2025

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Euphlebotomus
Statusvalid
DistributionSingapore (coastal NE) | Laos: Bokeo, Luangnamtha, Luangphabang, Vientiane Capital, Vientiane province (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, Table 2); printed as 'Ph. argentipes-like (Ph. seowpohi)'
Diagnostic notesClose to P. argentipes. Longer ascoids (asc/f2 0.71 ♀, 0.61 ♂), no sensilla on f3, one post-alar seta, ♀ pharyngeal armature colourless, f3/labrum about 1.0. Not in any printed key in this collection. | Not listed in Galati et al. 2025 (species described in Ding et al. 2025). | Lao specimens resemble Ph. argentipes morphologically but are genetically divergent (cyt-b); identified as Ph. seowpohi (Vongphayloth et al. 2026).
SourcesDing et al. 2025, Galati et al. 2025, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
