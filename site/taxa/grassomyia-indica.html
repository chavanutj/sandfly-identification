Grassomyia indica (Theodor)

ClassificationGenus Grassomyia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSergentomyia indica
ThailandChiang Mai, Loei, Nong Khai, as 'squamipleuris' (Quate 1962); Grassomyia sp. at Pha Tong (Renaux Torres et al. 2023)
Diagnostic notes♀ convex cibarial tooth row and round, finely spiculate spermatheca; ♂ genital filaments with dilated tips (Lewis 1978). Dark part of pigment belt straight anteriorly (Lewis 1987).
SourcesLewis 1978, Lewis 1987, Quate 1962, Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Grassomyia: females (Lewis 1987) – couplet 6b
  1. 1b Femora without short spines
  2. 3b Anepimeron without scales
  3. 6b Dark part of pigment belt straight anteriorly. Oriental Region
Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 63a
  1. 1b Cibarial teeth in a transverse row (Fig. 65), fore teeth sometimes present (Figs 136, 245) and usually pointing upward. Pigment patch (Figs 66, 136) usually present. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 usually with all or most hairs recumbent, most sockets much smaller than on 1 except in S. musai. Style of ♂ with four major spines and an accessory seta (Fig. 80) ⚠
  2. 48b Abdominal tergites 2-6 with few or no erect hairs
  3. 49b Abdominal tergites 2-6 usually with no erect hairs. Spermatheca not segmented but sometimes striated or wrinkled
  4. 63a Spermatheca a round finely spiculate capsule. Cibarium of ♀ with convex comb of pointed teeth. Pharynx of ♀ bulging near hind end. Antenna of ♀ with one ascoid on segments 4-15. Abdominal tergites 2-6 with some posterior erect hairs. Genital filaments with rounded expanded tips. Parameres blunt ⚠
Indo-Chinese species (Thailand to Viet Nam) (Quate 1962) – couplet 9b
  1. 1b Abdominal hairs on tergites 2 to 6 recumbent, sockets much smaller than on tergite 1; palpal segment 4 longer than 1/2 length of 3; ♂ dististyle with all spines distad of center (except sylvestris and khawi); pharynx armed or unarmed; cibarium of ♀ usually with well developed teeth ⚠
  2. 4a R2+3 (beta) subequal to or longer than R2 (alpha); delta 1/2 or less length of R2
  3. 5a Males
  4. 6b Dististyle with all spines apical or subapical, coxite not elongate; antennal segment 3 shorter than or not greatly exceeding proboscis
  5. 8a Antennal segment 3 ends before proboscis tip
  6. 9b Paramere ends in bluntly rounded apex; pharynx expanded distally and armed with few spines apically; Newstead scales on palpal segments 2 and 3; ridge bearing cibarial teeth straight or convex; pleuron with scales ⚠
Indo-Chinese species (Thailand to Viet Nam) (Quate 1962) – couplet 14a
  1. 1b Abdominal hairs on tergites 2 to 6 recumbent, sockets much smaller than on tergite 1; palpal segment 4 longer than 1/2 length of 3; ♂ dististyle with all spines distad of center (except sylvestris and khawi); pharynx armed or unarmed; cibarium of ♀ usually with well developed teeth ⚠
  2. 4a R2+3 (beta) subequal to or longer than R2 (alpha); delta 1/2 or less length of R2
  3. 5b Females
  4. 13a Pharynx armed with thick cluster of spines apically
  5. 14a Pigment patch terminating anteriorly as single point; Newstead scales on palpal segments 2 and 3; spermatheca spherical with many setose annulations and thick apical knob ⚠
Sri Lanka: females (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S3a
  1. G1b Wings narrow, lanceolate, symmetrical along the length (Fig. 1e). Cibarium with one or more rows of teeth, pigment patch usually present (Fig. 1c)
  2. S1a Spermatheca with capsule (Fig. 4b, c), not tubular
  3. S2a Capsule of spermatheca with numerous spicules or striations (Fig. 4b, c)
  4. S3a Tip of spermatheca with minute projections (Fig. 4b)
Sri Lanka: males (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S5a
  1. G1b Style with 4-5 spines, usually terminal; if not all spines terminal, 2 spines terminal and 2 sub-terminal, often in pairs (Fig. 1g)
  2. S1b Aedeagus gradually tapering to the end (Fig. 2f)
  3. S4a Genital filaments with dilated ends; A3 without ascoid
  4. S5a Paramere with rounded end (Fig. 4h)
