Idiophlebotomus species (Lewis 1982) – couplet F3a
  1. 0a Female
  2. F1a Cibarial armature with median rod or big tooth
  3. F2b Cibarial median rod with minute serrations or none
  4. F3a Cibarium without patch of teeth except a few granulose spicules
Idiophlebotomus species (Lewis 1982) – couplet M3a
  1. 0b Male
  2. M1b Apical spine of style without such expansion
  3. M2a Style with three spines
  4. M3a Coxite with the two non-distal spines at 0.39. Paramere nearly straight with bulbous apex
