Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 19a
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well ⚠
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15a Coxite of ♂ with sub-basal hairy process. Genital filaments 1.3-2.3 times as long as pump
  4. 16b Coxite 0.20-0.33 mm long, its process usually large and having a brush of long hairs. Style not long, with four or five spines. Paramere simple, distal upper surface flat and elliptical with short hairs. Surstyle without terminal spines. Pharynx of ♀ with teeth or scales. Spermatheca sometimes with differentiated rounded end-segment
  5. 19a Style with five long spines, two at the tip and two near the middle. Pharynx of ♀ with irregular scales or punctiform teeth
