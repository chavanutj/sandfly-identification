Phlebotomus maynei Sinton, 1930

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Anaphlebotomus
StatusReinstated as valid (Renaux Torres et al. 2023); previously a synonym of P. stantoni
DistributionIndia (Saharanpur) | Records in the project regions: India (Galati et al. 2025)
Diagnostic notes♂ aedeagal ducts of intermediate length (about 448 µm in holotype) between P. stantoni and P. shadenae.
SourcesRenaux Torres et al. 2023, Galati et al. 2025
