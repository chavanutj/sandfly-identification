Sergentomyia hodgsoni hodgsoni (Sinton)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Neophlebotomus
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSergentomyia hodgsoni
DistributionRecords in the project regions: India, Malaysia, Pakistan, Thailand (Galati et al. 2025, as S. hodgsoni)
ThailandThailand (Galati et al. 2025, as S. hodgsoni) | Ranong, Chumphon, Uthaithani, Chiang Rai, Sakon Nakhon (as S. hodgsoni, subspecies not stated) (Polseela et al. 2024); Tham Pha Thai, Lampang (as Se. hodgsoni, subspecies not stated) (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3) (as S. hodgsoni)
Diagnostic notesGalati et al. 2025 (p. 28) list it as Sergentomyia (Neophlebotomus) hodgsoni (Sinton, 1933).
SourcesLewis 1978, Galati et al. 2025, Polseela et al. 2024, Sunantaraporn et al. 2024, Soe et al. 2026
