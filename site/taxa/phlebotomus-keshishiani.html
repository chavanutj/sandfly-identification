Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 43b
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15b Coxite of ♂ without such process. Genital filaments 3-11 times as long as pump
  4. 24b Style with five long spines
  5. 30b Paramere without ventral process. Pharyngeal armature otherwise ⚠
  6. 38b Paramere not truncated
  7. 39a Pharynx of ♀ and ♂ with punctiform teeth. Spermatheca segmented, with long finger-like process. Genital filaments 3-5 times as long as pump
  8. 40a Female
  9. 41b Pharynx with readily visible spicules
  10. 42b Spermatheca with 8-21 segments
  11. 43b Spermatheca narrowing at one or both ends, with about 18-21 segments
