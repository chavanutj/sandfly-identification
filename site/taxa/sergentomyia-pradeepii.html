Sergentomyia pradeepii Shah et al., 2025

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Neophlebotomus
Statusvalid
DistributionIndia: Madhya Pradesh. Type locality: cattle sheds and adjacent rooms, Johariya village, tehsil Sagar, gram panchayat Masurhai, Sagar district, 23.78 N 78.52 E, 534 m; collected 24-25 July 2023 (Shah et al. 2025).
Diagnostic notes♀: labrum 245 µm; hypopharynx almost smooth; maxilla with 3-5 internal and 40-44 external teeth (as printed); palpal formula 5,4,3,2,1; ascoid on f2 59 µm; cibarium with 29-31 pointed hind teeth in a tapering row (27-30 in the diagnosis), about 11 fore teeth/denticles (9-11 in the diagnosis), dark pear-shaped pigment patch with a pointed tip; pharynx pot-shaped, 155 x 61 µm, heavily armed with long spicules; spermatheca cylindrical, thick-walled, 61 x 29 µm, faint striations only at the tip, short cylindrical terminal knob; ducts short or not visible. ♂: labrum 196 µm; cibarium with 23-27 rudimentary hind teeth and 6-9 indistinct fore teeth, pigment patch less distinct; gonostyle 119 µm with two apical and two subapical spines (23 µm apart) and an accessory spine just below the subapical spine; gonocoxite 272 µm with median tuft of 55-60 setae; paramere hooked, 176 µm, about 30 setae; parameral sheath tapered with a blunt end; sperm pump 107 µm, aedeagal ducts 310 µm, striated. Compared species (values as given by Shah et al. 2025): S. ashwanii (♀ 10-12 cibarial teeth, 4-6 denticles, funnel-shaped pigment patch), S. dhandai (♀ 24 pointed teeth, broad pigment patch, thick-walled spermatheca with faint wrinkles and short cylindrical knob; ♂ about 24 teeth, no pigment patch), S. gemmea (♀ 10 tapering teeth, pale pigment patch), S. iyengari (♀ 20 teeth, central ones smaller), S. monticola (♀ 10 teeth, 3-4 denticles) and S. hodgsoni (♀ 50-60 teeth; ♂ spinose process on paramere). COI distance 13.4% overall to congeners; 13.8% to S. iyengari, 14.8% to S. ashwanii. Not in any printed key: in Lewis 1978 the ♀ tooth count falls between couplet 127a (50-60) and 127b (about 20 or less); spermatheca as in S. dhandai (121a).
SourcesShah et al. 2025
