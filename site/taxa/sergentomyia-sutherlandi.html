Sergentomyia sutherlandi Vongphayloth, Randrianambinintsoa & Depaquit, 2026

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid; described as n. sp. (female only)
DistributionLaos: Luangnamtha only (17 females); type locality Kao Lao cave (20.7238N, 101.1533E), Viengphukha district, Luangnamtha province (Vongphayloth et al. 2026)
Diagnostic notesS. barraudi-like comb cibarium; closest to S. maiae and S. mahadevani (Vongphayloth et al. 2026). ♀ cibarium with 73-78 palisadic posterior teeth (mean 76.5, n = 4; holotype 77) and two rows of anterior teeth (row 1: 10-14, row 2: 10-12; holotype ten per row); sclerotized area thick with a broad fore end (width 53-59 µm, height to top 41.8-49 µm, base 16-26 µm). S. maiae: 57-69 posterior teeth, anterior rows 8-13 and 9-12, thin sclerotized area with narrow fore end (width 37-55 µm, height 24-34 µm, base 8-15 µm). S. mahadevani: about 50 posterior teeth, broad fore end but no anterior teeth. Other values (sutherlandi vs maiae): f1 312.86-365 vs 330.08-418.38 µm; f1/(f2+f3) 1.17-1.3 vs 1.26-1.29; clypeus 100-137 vs 100.45-166.73 µm; wing β (r2+3) 189.07-209 vs 213.02-270.21 µm; α/β 3.23-3.7 vs 1.02-2.76 (Table 4). Palpal formula 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; about 40 Newstead's sensilla on p3; ascoids 2/f3-f13; spermathecae smooth, elongate, capsule-like. Cyt-b distance to S. maiae 12%. Male unknown. Not in any printed key in this collection. Note: the paper's text cites Fig 3D/3F for S. sutherlandi/S. maiae, but the Fig 3 caption labels E = S. maiae paratype, F = S. sutherlandi.
