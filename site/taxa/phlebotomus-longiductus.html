Phlebotomus longiductus Parrot, 1928

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Adlerius
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: China, India, Pakistan (Galati et al. 2025) | Bhutan: widespread, 29.4% of Phlebotomus (Wangdi et al. 2024)
ImportanceCited as a proven vector of visceral leishmaniasis in China and Central Asia and a known vector of L. infantum in the Himalayan regions of India, also found with L. tropica (Wangdi et al. 2024).
SourcesLewis 1978, Akhoundi et al. 2025, Galati et al. 2025, Wangdi et al. 2024
