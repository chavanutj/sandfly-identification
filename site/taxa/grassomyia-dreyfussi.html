Grassomyia dreyfussi (Parrot, 1933)

ClassificationGenus Grassomyia
StatusSri Lankan records refer to G. dreyfussi turkestanica (Gajapathy & Surendran 2012)
Other names in keysSergentomyia dreyfussi
SourcesWijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Lewis 1987, Gajapathy & Surendran 2012

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Sri Lanka: females (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S3b
  1. G1b Wings narrow, lanceolate, symmetrical along the length (Fig. 1e). Cibarium with one or more rows of teeth, pigment patch usually present (Fig. 1c)
  2. S1a Spermatheca with capsule (Fig. 4b, c), not tubular
  3. S2a Capsule of spermatheca with numerous spicules or striations (Fig. 4b, c)
  4. S3b Tip of spermatheca with small projections (Fig. 4c)
Sri Lanka: males (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020) – couplet S5b
  1. G1b Style with 4-5 spines, usually terminal; if not all spines terminal, 2 spines terminal and 2 sub-terminal, often in pairs (Fig. 1g)
  2. S1b Aedeagus gradually tapering to the end (Fig. 2f)
  3. S4a Genital filaments with dilated ends; A3 without ascoid
  4. S5b Paramere with hooked end (Fig. 4i)
