Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 32a
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15b Coxite of ♂ without such process. Genital filaments 3-11 times as long as pump
  4. 24b Style with five long spines
  5. 30a Paramere with three lobes (two in an Iranian species). Pharynx of ♀ as in Anaphlebotomus. Spermatheca with differentiated end-segment ⚠
  6. 31a Female
  7. 32a Spermatheca with faint transverse striations
Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 35a
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15b Coxite of ♂ without such process. Genital filaments 3-11 times as long as pump
  4. 24b Style with five long spines
  5. 30a Paramere with three lobes (two in an Iranian species). Pharynx of ♀ as in Anaphlebotomus. Spermatheca with differentiated end-segment ⚠
  6. 31b Male
  7. 35a Middle lobe of paramere thicker than main (upper) lobe
Euphlebotomus species (argentipes group) (Lewis 1982) – couplet F3b
  1. 0a Female
  2. F1b Spermatheca not bulbous, without narrow neck. Pharyngeal armature comprising distinct teeth or ridges
  3. F2a Spermatheca with faint transverse striations or indistinct segments
  4. F3b Spermatheca with small apical segment and about 30 transverse striations. Pharynx with a median group of small teeth and, behind them, some close-set concentric lines
Euphlebotomus species (argentipes group) (Lewis 1982) – couplet M3a
  1. 0b Male
  2. M1b Paramere with three lobes
  3. M2b Middle lobe of paramere with rounded end
  4. M3a Middle lobe of paramere thicker than main (upper) lobe
