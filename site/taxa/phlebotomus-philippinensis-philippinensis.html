Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 34a
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15b Coxite of ♂ without such process. Genital filaments 3-11 times as long as pump
  4. 24b Style with five long spines
  5. 30a Paramere with three lobes (two in an Iranian species). Pharynx of ♀ as in Anaphlebotomus. Spermatheca with differentiated end-segment ⚠
  6. 31a Female
  7. 32b Spermatheca distinctly segmented
  8. 33b Spermathecal common duct with thick walls. Antenna 5 with papilla
  9. 34a Antenna 3/labrum 1.0
Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 37a
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15b Coxite of ♂ without such process. Genital filaments 3-11 times as long as pump
  4. 24b Style with five long spines
  5. 30a Paramere with three lobes (two in an Iranian species). Pharynx of ♀ as in Anaphlebotomus. Spermatheca with differentiated end-segment ⚠
  6. 31b Male
  7. 35b Middle lobe of paramere thinner than main lobe
  8. 36b Main lobe of paramere about twice or more length of middle lobe, lower lobe appearing narrow but extending mesally, depth of paramere about 0.35 of its length
  9. 37a Antenna 3/labrum 1.7. Style 0.54 length of coxite, and 4.1 times as thick as long
Euphlebotomus species (argentipes group) (Lewis 1982) – couplet F5a
  1. 0a Female
  2. F1b Spermatheca not bulbous, without narrow neck. Pharyngeal armature comprising distinct teeth or ridges
  3. F2b Spermatheca distinctly segmented
  4. F4b Spermathecal common duct with thick walls. Antenna 5 with papilla
  5. F5a Antenna 3/labrum 1.0
Euphlebotomus species (argentipes group) (Lewis 1982) – couplet M6a
  1. 0b Male
  2. M1b Paramere with three lobes
  3. M2b Middle lobe of paramere with rounded end
  4. M3b Middle lobe of paramere thinner than main lobe
  5. M4b Main lobe of paramere about twice length of middle lobe
  6. M5b Aedeagus with accessory spines. Lower lobe of paramere appearing narrow but extending mesally, depth of paramere about 0.35 of its length
  7. M6a Antenna 3/labrum 1.7. Style 0.54 length of coxite, and more than four times as long as thick
