Sergentomyia rudnicki Lewis

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Parrotomyia
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: Indonesia, Malaysia, Sri Lanka (Galati et al. 2025) | Laos: karstic limestone areas of Vientiane Province (Vongphayloth et al. 2024)
ThailandChumphon, Phayao (Polseela et al. 2024); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesGalati et al. 2025 (p. 38): the record from Sri Lanka needs validation. | Laos specimens: f1 > 250 µm, beyond tip of proboscis; about 80 comb-like teeth and two rows of more than 30 anterior teeth each (original description about 90 teeth, two rows of about 20); oblong annulate spermathecae (Vongphayloth et al. 2024). | Described from Malaysia: about 90 comb-like cibarial teeth, two rows of about 22 vertical teeth, long pharyngeal teeth. Lao Se. rudnicki and Se. brevicaulis differ by only 1.4% in cyt-b (Vongphayloth et al. 2026); see S. brevicaulis.
SourcesLewis 1978, Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Galati et al. 2025, Polseela et al. 2024, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2024, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
