Phlebotomus shadenae Depaquit, Pellot & Siriyasatien, 2023

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Anaphlebotomus
Statusvalid
DistributionNorthern and southern Thailand; Laos | Records in the project regions: Laos, Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Laos: Luangnamtha (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, Table 2)
ThailandPha Tong cave, Chiang Rai (Renaux Torres et al. 2023) | Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesClose to P. stantoni. ♂ aedeagal ducts very long (640-671 µm), gonocoxite with median tuft of about 56 setae, accessory spine between paramere and parameral sheath. ♀ spermathecal common duct about 508 µm, individual ducts about 69 µm. Not in any printed key in this collection.
SourcesRenaux Torres et al. 2023, Galati et al. 2025, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
