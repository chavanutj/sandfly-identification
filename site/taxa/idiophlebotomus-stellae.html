Idiophlebotomus species (Lewis 1982) – couplet M5b
  1. 0b Male
  2. M1b Apical spine of style without such expansion
  3. M2b Style with more than three spines
  4. M4b Style with five spines
  5. M5b Aedeagus small and triangular. Paramere with basal dorsal curved appendage
