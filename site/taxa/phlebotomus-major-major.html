Phlebotomus major major Annandale, 1910

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Larroussius
Statusvalid
Other names in keysPhlebotomus major
DistributionRecords in the project regions: India, Nepal, Pakistan (Galati et al. 2025, as P. major) | Bhutan, recorded as 'P. major' (subspecies not stated): Jangbi, Jurmed, Kakpadung, Kengkhar, low numbers (Wangdi et al. 2024)
ThailandUthaithani (18 females) (Polseela et al. 2024); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3) (as P. major)
Diagnostic notesGalati et al. 2025 (p. 25) list it as Phlebotomus (Larroussius) major Annandale, 1910. Galati et al. 2025 (p. 38): P. major is a species complex (P. neglectus, P. syriacus, P. wenyoni, P. major s.s.); records of P. major s.s. in Southeastern Asia appear erroneous. | Vongphayloth et al. 2023 (p. 16): several records of Ph. major in Southeast Asia are 'probably irrelevant and certainly refer to Ph. betisi'; re-examination recommended (see conflicts).
SourcesLewis 1978, Akhoundi et al. 2025, Galati et al. 2025, Wangdi et al. 2024, Polseela et al. 2024, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2023
