Sergentomyia mahadevani Lewis, 1978

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: Thailand (Galati et al. 2025)
ThailandThailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Phayao, Chiang Rai (Polseela et al. 2024)
Diagnostic notesCompare S. sutherlandi (Laos): 73-78 posterior cibarial teeth (S. mahadevani about 50) and two rows of anterior teeth (none in S. mahadevani) (Vongphayloth et al. 2026).
