Sergentomyia marolii Vongphayloth, Randrianambinintsoa & Depaquit, 2024

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionLaos: Vientiane Province (type locality Pha Nok Kok cave, Feung district); two females. Male unknown.
Diagnostic notes♀ f1 shorter than f2 + f3 (about 178 µm); labrum 228-256 µm. Cibarium with 11 big teeth along a very slightly curved line, no denticles (anterior teeth) according to the description (Table 3 lists the 11 as anterior teeth); sclerotized area strongly pigmented, thick and narrow. Pharynx strongly armed with pointed translucent teeth oriented backwards (posterior quarter). Mesofurca dark brown, strongly pigmented. Spermathecae short, wrinkled, oblong (30-35 x 18 µm), terminal knob not embedded; individual ducts about 160 µm. Close to Se. bigtii (Philippines; 10-12 cibarial teeth, long pharyngeal teeth) but f1 shorter (Vongphayloth et al. 2024).
SourcesVongphayloth et al. 2024
