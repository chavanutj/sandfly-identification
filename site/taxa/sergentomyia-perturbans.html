Sergentomyia perturbans (de Meijere, 1909)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Neophlebotomus
StatusIdentity doubtful (Ding et al. 2025); sylvestris Sinton and whartoni Lewis synonymised by Lewis 1978
Other names in keysSergentomyia perturbans s.l.
DistributionRecords in the project regions: Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam (Galati et al. 2025) | Laos: Vientiane province (1 female) (Vongphayloth et al. 2026, Table 2)
ThailandThailand (Galati et al. 2025) | Kanchanaburi (Ma Glue and Phra caves) (Soomro et al. 2025); listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesGalati et al. 2025 (p. 31) list it as Sergentomyia (Rondanomyia) perturbans (de Meijere, 1909). Galati et al. 2025 (p. 38): identity doubtful due to its confused history, particularly outside Indonesia. They list S. (Rondanomyia) sylvestris (Sinton, 1924) (India; p. 32) and S. (Neophlebotomus) whartoni Lewis, 1957 (p. 28) as valid species. | Laos specimens identified after Lewis 1978: pharynx with faint ridges (unarmed), mesofurca strongly pigmented; the Laos record is considered uncertain (Vongphayloth et al. 2024).
SourcesLewis 1978, Quate 1962, Ding et al. 2025, Galati et al. 2025, Soomro et al. 2025, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2024, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
