Sergentomyia sp. 1 (Soomro et al. 2025)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia
Statusundescribed
DistributionThailand (Kanchanaburi caves)
ThailandKanchanaburi: Phra, Taklor and Manow Phee caves (Soomro et al. 2025)
Diagnostic notesFemale only (Soomro et al. 2025, Fig. 2): ventral plate of cibarium with 15-16 cibarial (hind) teeth; dorsal plate with a funnel-shaped patch of dark golden-brown pigment; spermatheca tubular, segmented into 8-10 parts, with secretory cells at its distal end; spermathecal duct elongated, broadening toward its end; pharynx slender with relatively wide base and a robust array of pointed translucent teeth along the central axis in its posterior quarter. Molecular: distinct clade (bootstrap 100%); COI identity 90% or less to Se. anodontis, Cytb 89% or less to Se. boironis. Not formally described.
SourcesSoomro et al. 2025
