Euphlebotomus species (argentipes group) (Lewis 1982) – couplet F3a
  1. 0a Female
  2. F1b Spermatheca not bulbous, without narrow neck. Pharyngeal armature comprising distinct teeth or ridges
  3. F2a Spermatheca with faint transverse striations or indistinct segments
  4. F3a Spermatheca with large apical segment and about 15 indistinct segments. Pharynx with many uniform teeth ⚠
