Sergentomyia zeylanica (Annandale, 1910)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Neophlebotomus
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: India, Sri Lanka (Galati et al. 2025) | Sri Lanka: specimens referred to the University of Kelaniya in 2024 from Gampaha, Galle and Anuradhapura districts (districts per species not given), n=21 (36.2%) of 58 examined (Kumari et al. 2026)
Diagnostic notesGalati et al. 2025 (p. 32) list it as Sergentomyia (Rondanomyia) zeylanica (Annandale, 1910). | Compare S. chattiensis (Shah et al. 2026; COI distance 12.3%): ♀ 15-18 hind teeth and about eight rows of fore teeth (three rows in S. zeylanica); ♂ gonocoxite with a median tuft of 11-13 setae.
SourcesLewis 1978, Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Galati et al. 2025, Shah et al. 2026, Kumari et al. 2026
