Sergentomyia maiae Depaquit, Renaux Torres & Siriyasatien, 2023

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, ungrouped
Statusvalid
DistributionThailand | Records in the project regions: Thailand (Galati et al. 2025)
ThailandPha Tong cave, Chiang Rai (Renaux Torres et al. 2023) | Thailand (Galati et al. 2025) | listed in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesS. barraudi group. ♀ cibarium with 57-69 palisade-like teeth plus two rows of vertical teeth (8-13 and 6-9), the lower row set very low in the pigmented sclerotized area. Male unknown. | Compare S. sutherlandi (Laos): 73-78 posterior cibarial teeth, thick sclerotized area with broad fore end; cyt-b distance 12% (Vongphayloth et al. 2026).
SourcesRenaux Torres et al. 2023, Galati et al. 2025, Soe et al. 2026, Vongphayloth et al. 2026
