Sergentomyia dentata (Sinton, 1933)

ClassificationGenus Sergentomyia, subgenus Sergentomyia
Statusvalid
DistributionRecords in the project regions: India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka (Galati et al. 2025)
Thailandlisted in the 42-species Thai checklist (Soe et al. 2026, Table 3)
Diagnostic notesPolseela et al. (2016) state that the record of S. dentata in Thailand (Polseela et al. 2011) should be checked against S. phadangensis, which has larger, darker cibarial teeth and a narrow pharynx with discrete armature; S. dentata is mainly Middle Eastern / Eastern Mediterranean.
SourcesLewis 1978, Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020, Galati et al. 2025, Polseela et al. 2016, Soe et al. 2026
