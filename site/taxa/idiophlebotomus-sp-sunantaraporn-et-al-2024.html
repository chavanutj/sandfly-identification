Idiophlebotomus sp. (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024)

ClassificationGenus Idiophlebotomus
Statusundescribed
DistributionThailand (Lampang)
ThailandTham Pha Thai cave, Lampang, 2 females (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024)
Diagnostic notesFemale (specimen PT312) compared with the Id. teshi holotype from Nepal (Sunantaraporn et al. 2024, Fig. 5): spermathecae very similar (sclerotized distal part); lateral cibarial teeth almost perfectly aligned as in Id. teshi but the V-structure much less marked; Newstead's sensilla of palp segment 3 on a distal process (proximal process in Id. teshi); antennae missing. COI 96.27% identity to Id. teshi. Authors withheld a name pending the male of Id. teshi and molecular data from Nepal.
SourcesSunantaraporn et al. 2024
