Idiophlebotomus sp. (Kumari et al. 2026)

ClassificationGenus Idiophlebotomus
Statusundescribed
DistributionSri Lanka, first record of the genus: Gampaha district (7.279 164°N, 80.080 019°E; 7.221 161 6°N, 80.065 118 6°E; 7.231 812°N, 80.152 155°E) and Galle district, Southern Province (6.269 509°N, 80.293 887°E); 5 males, no females (M:F 5:0), 2024; forested habitats rich in leaf litter and rocky substrates (Kumari et al. 2026). GenBank COI PV718003, PV718009.
Diagnostic notes♂ only (Kumari et al. 2026, pp. 275-277): genus recognised by cibarium bearing teeth, unarmed pharynx, palps not extending beyond A3, style with 3 spines and a pair of intra-abdominal rods parallel to the pump and genital filaments. A3 longer than A4 + A5; cibarium with numerous denticles in multiple distinct rows; palpal formula 1,4,2,5,3; style elongate and slender with three pointed spines, neither terminal nor subterminal, spaced apart; coxite broad with numerous long setae, dense apically; paramere narrow and elongate, without dorsal or ventral processes; aedeagus darkly pigmented with slightly swollen tip; wing length/width 3.08; pharynx slender with fine scales (as printed, besides 'unarmed'). Measurements (µm, n=2; antenna, palp and leg from one specimen): head 258.12-263.37 x 318.00-386.79; antenna (Table 1 rows 'A1', 'A2', 'A3') 487.49, 160.42, 149.68; P1-P5 15.02, 43.18, 71.86, 35.50, 61.96; wing 1567.57-1580.24 x 497.52-524.79; style 285.07-337.24; coxite 224.96-274.50 x 90.98-100.16; individual surstyle 255.02-302.77; paramere 230.64-240.97; aedeagus 103.94-126.43; genital filaments + pump 464.79-471.10; genital pump 144.28-206.34. Molecular doubt: COI showed about 92% similarity to Micropygomyia atroclavata (BLAST); the two sequences did not cluster together and no Idiophlebotomus group was recovered, so the authors treat the specimens conservatively as Idiophlebotomus sp. Not in the Sri Lanka keys (Wijerathna & Gunathilaka 2020), which cover only Phlebotomus and Sergentomyia; use the Lewis 1982 Idiophlebotomus key.
