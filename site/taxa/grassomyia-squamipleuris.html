Grassomyia squamipleuris (Newstead, 1912)

ClassificationGenus Grassomyia
Statusvalid
Other names in keysSergentomyia squamipleuris
DistributionTaiwan, as Se. squamipleuris: 17 of 76 positive sites (Chung et al. 2025)
Diagnostic notesTaiwan females: 30-35 posterior cibarial teeth; pharynx posteriorly expanded and densely armed with prominent teeth. COI/cytb cluster with Se. indica; proposed as probably the same species (Chung et al. 2025).
SourcesLewis 1987, Galati et al. 2025, Chung et al. 2025
