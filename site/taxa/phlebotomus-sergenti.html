Phlebotomus sergenti Parrot

ClassificationGenus Phlebotomus, subgenus Paraphlebotomus
Statusvalid
SourcesLewis 1978, Dantas-Torres et al. 2014

How it keys out

The chain of characters leading to this taxon in each key (a ready-made diagnosis).

Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 21b
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15a Coxite of ♂ with sub-basal hairy process. Genital filaments 1.3-2.3 times as long as pump
  4. 16b Coxite 0.20-0.33 mm long, its process usually large and having a brush of long hairs. Style not long, with four or five spines. Paramere simple, distal upper surface flat and elliptical with short hairs. Surstyle without terminal spines. Pharynx of ♀ with teeth or scales. Spermatheca sometimes with differentiated rounded end-segment
  5. 19b Style with four long spines, two near the tip and two near the base. Pharynx of ♀ with large backwardly directed teeth
  6. 20a Female
  7. 21b Antenna 3 long (0.23-0.33 mm), 0.7-1.0 length of labrum
Oriental Phlebotominae: species and subspecies (Lewis 1978) – couplet 23b
  1. 1a Cibarial teeth absent or, if present, usually in the form of spicules and not in a definite row (Fig. 27). Pigment patch nearly always absent. Hind ends of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect hairs (Fig. 1), sockets as large as on 1. Style of ♂ with three to five spines (Figs 19, 25) and sometimes (some species of subgenus Idiophlebotomus) with two hairs as well
  2. 2b Cibarium with spicules or unarmed. Pharynx usually armed. Palp extending further than antenna 3
  3. 15a Coxite of ♂ with sub-basal hairy process. Genital filaments 1.3-2.3 times as long as pump
  4. 16b Coxite 0.20-0.33 mm long, its process usually large and having a brush of long hairs. Style not long, with four or five spines. Paramere simple, distal upper surface flat and elliptical with short hairs. Surstyle without terminal spines. Pharynx of ♀ with teeth or scales. Spermatheca sometimes with differentiated rounded end-segment
  5. 19b Style with four long spines, two near the tip and two near the base. Pharynx of ♀ with large backwardly directed teeth
  6. 20b Male
  7. 22b Basal process of coxite small and thin with hairs only at its end
  8. 23b Antenna 3 long (0.25-0.34 mm), 1.0-1.4 length of labrum. Genital pump long (0.17-0.2 mm) with broad basal plate
Italy (comparison key) (Dantas-Torres et al. 2014) – couplet M2a
  1. G1b Cibarial teeth and pigment patch usually absent (Fig. 1C). Hind end of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect setae, which arise from large sockets of the same size as those on tergite 1 (Fig. 1D). Male style with 2 long apical spines (Fig. 2B, D-H) or 3 short apical spines (Fig. 2C) and setiform spine absent. Female spermathecae with superficial striation (Fig. 3B) or annulated (Fig. 3C-H)
  2. G2a Male
  3. M1a Coxite (= basistyle) with bristles in the basal region
  4. M2a Style (= dististyle) short with 4 long spines: 2 apical and the internal more basal than the external spine. Paramere without dorsal ramification. Surstyle (= lateral lobe) without short distal spines (Fig. 2B)
Italy (comparison key) (Dantas-Torres et al. 2014) – couplet F3b
  1. G1b Cibarial teeth and pigment patch usually absent (Fig. 1C). Hind end of abdominal tergites 2-6 with many erect setae, which arise from large sockets of the same size as those on tergite 1 (Fig. 1D). Male style with 2 long apical spines (Fig. 2B, D-H) or 3 short apical spines (Fig. 2C) and setiform spine absent. Female spermathecae with superficial striation (Fig. 3B) or annulated (Fig. 3C-H)
  2. G2b Female
  3. F1b Spermathecae ringed. Pharynx not as above
  4. F2a Spermathecae without neck
  5. F3b Spermathecae with 4-5 rings. Pharyngeal armature occupying about a quarter length of pharynx, with few large teeth directed backward (Figs 3D, 4D)
